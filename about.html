Our Story

Why We Built UniPath KH

Every year, thousands of students graduate from high school in Cambodia with no clear idea of where to go next. They don't know what universities exist, what majors are offered, how much tuition costs, or whether scholarships are available.

Most students rely on word of mouth — which means they often miss great opportunities simply because they didn't have the information. We built UniPath KH to fix that. Our goal is simple: give every Cambodian student access to clear, organized university information so they can make the best decision for their future.

UniPath KH is a student project built for INFO 250 — Web Development I at the American University of Phnom Penh, Spring 2026.
